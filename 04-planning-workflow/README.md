# 04 - Planning, Prioritization & Crisis Management

## 📌 Overview
This module demonstrates prompt structures for analyzing high-pressure operational scenarios, defining minimum acceptable scopes (MVP), and resolving resource allocation trade-offs.

## 🛠️ Workflows & Prompts

### Overloaded Inbox Triaging & Crisis Plan
- **Task Type:** Analyze + Plan
- **Purpose:** Evaluates high-pressure launch scenarios to extract minimum acceptable scopes (MVP) and assign team ownership.
- **Prompt:**
Context: Returning from 2 days OOO with 30 unread messages and a critical launch deadline in 5 days.
Action: Analyze the situation, resolve the 4 key launch decisions, and distribute action items across teams.
Role: Senior Product Manager expert in crisis management.
Expected Output: A 4-decision resolution summary + a 5-column team allocation table.
